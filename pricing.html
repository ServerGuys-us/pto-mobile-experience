❓ FAQ

Pricing Questions

Everything on the bus is included — professional chauffeur, fuel, all lighting and sound systems, onboard amenities, and route planning assistance. You only pay for any add-ons or beverages you request separately.
Yes, we require a 25% deposit at the time of booking to secure your date and vehicle. The remaining balance is due 48 hours before your event. We accept all major credit cards via Stripe.
Cancellations made 30+ days before your event receive a full deposit refund. Cancellations 14–29 days prior receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 14 days of the event are non-refundable. However, we're always happy to reschedule at no charge with 7+ days notice.
Gratuity is not included in the listed rate. Our drivers work incredibly hard to make your experience perfect and a 15–20% tip is customary and greatly appreciated.
Yes, premium rates may apply for New Year's Eve, prom season (April–May), homecoming season, Labor Day, Fourth of July, and other high-demand periods. We recommend booking 4–6 weeks in advance for holiday dates.
Yes! You are welcome to bring your own beverages. The bus includes a full bar setup with ice coolers. For guests 21+, we also offer a curated beverage package add-on. All passengers must comply with applicable alcohol laws.
Overtime is billed at the standard hourly rate for that vehicle, in 1-hour increments. We'll let you know when you're approaching your scheduled end time and you can extend on the spot if the vehicle is available.
