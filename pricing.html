Pricing FAQ

Every package includes your professional chauffeur, fuel, LED lighting, premium sound system, VIP lounge seating, and route planning. Add-ons are available separately — see the list above.
Yes, we require a deposit at the time of booking to secure your date. The remaining balance is due before your event. We accept all major credit cards and other payment methods — contact us for details.
Cancellations made 30+ days before your event receive a full deposit refund. Cancellations 14–29 days prior receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable. We're always happy to reschedule with sufficient notice.
Gratuity is not included in the package rate. Our drivers work incredibly hard to make your experience perfect — a 15–20% tip is customary and greatly appreciated.
Yes! You are welcome to bring your own beverages. We also offer a Bottle Package add-on ($300) for a curated experience. All passengers must comply with applicable laws and regulations.
Overtime is billed at a pro-rated rate based on your package. We'll give you a heads up as you approach your scheduled end time. Extension is subject to vehicle availability.
